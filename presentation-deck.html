01 • Problem Analysis
Sub-Saharan Agricultural Crisis

The Vicious Cycle of Erratic Rainfall

Smallholder farmers face unpredictable weather, severe soil degradation, and complete financial exclusion.

⚠️ Climate Chaos
The "False Start" Trap
Early isolated downpours trick farmers into premature sowing. A subsequent 14-day dry spell incinerates seedlings, wiping out annual investments and seed reserves.
100% Initial Seed Loss
📉 Soil Exhaustion
Continuous Monoculture
Repeated cereal monoculture (Maize-Maize) exhausts organic soil nitrogen (ΔN < -200 kg/ha) and stimulates parasitic weeds (Striga hermonthica) across 60% of northern fields.
-295 kg Nitrogen Deficit / Ha
🚫 Financial Exclusion
The Credit Desert
Because rainfall variability creates severe default risks, regional banks and microfinance institutions reject over 70% of agricultural credit applications.
> 70% Credit Rejection Rate
🛑 The Root Cause : Traditional weather apps only look at clouds in the sky. They fail to measure root-zone water in the soil where seeds live.
In West Africa, planting three days too early because of an isolated cloud burst can destroy a family for an entire year. Without crop rotation, soils collapse and banks refuse credit.

02 • NASA Earth Observations
Multi-Sensor Space Intelligence

Resolving the Mismatch : 36 km to 30 m

Fusing NASA microwave radiometry, thermal infrared, and digital elevation into field-scale root-zone intelligence.

🛰️ Real-Time NASA Datasets
Spaceborne Sensor Pipeline
• NASA POWER API (LaRC) : 30-day precipitation series (PRECTOTCORR), 2m temperatures (T2M), and consecutive dry spell detection (>8 days).
• NASA SMAP (SPL3SMP_E) : L-band microwave radiometer measuring 0–5 cm surface soil moisture through vegetation at 9–36 km.
• NASA ECOSTRESS (ECO_L2T_LSTE) : High-resolution (70m) thermal infrared Land Surface Temperature aboard the International Space Station.
• NASA SRTM : 30-meter Digital Elevation Model for local terrain slope and topographic wetness accumulation.
🔬 Biophysical Downscaling Algorithm
Physical Fusion Formulation

We downscale coarse microwave soil moisture by correlating evapotranspirative cooling and topographic gravity accumulation:

SM_30m = SM_coarse × [ 1.0 - α(TVDI - TVDI_ref) + β(TWI / TWI_ref) ]

• TVDI (Thermal Dryness) : Distinguishes hydrated vegetation from moisture-stressed crops.
• TWI (Topographic Wetness) : Identifies water retention pockets along field slopes.

30 Meters Field-Scale Resolution
Satellites like SMAP provide invaluable microwave moisture, but at 36 km. Terra-Orbit couples SMAP with ECOSTRESS thermal data and SRTM topography to deliver 30-meter parcel intelligence.
